# Structured scale/contact experiment — FAIL with useful evidence

The automatic planner now supplies separate scale_relationships and contacts
arrays, concatenated into both construction and the single-frame rendering brief.
All five real passes generated. The three platform benchmark examples compile.

Actual skeleton and final images inspected. The couch perspective is more
convincing and the final fur replaces most construction marks. However, two
tails are already present in the skeleton and persist in the final. The action
is not a clear landing on the cushion; rear feet appear supported while the
front body is airborne. Proportions/contact quality are not accepted.

This makes an early-pass failure explicit: later passes preserve and polish an
invalid body. Additional prose at the final pass cannot be assumed to correct
topology. A topology/contact review before advancing from construction is needed,
with rejection or a bounded repair, rather than automatic progression through
all five passes. The existing callback can reject a pass, but production has
not yet connected an actual visual reviewer at that boundary.

The structured fields are currently an experimental planner benchmark contract,
not new production API fields. No claim of a complete animation or artistic pass.
