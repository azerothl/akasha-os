# Body Construction

Construction connects primitive forms into a coherent body.

The mannequin remains the structural source.

## Objectives

Establish:

- connections between masses
- overlaps
- compression
- stretching
- body rhythm
- simplified silhouette

## Torso

Connect:

rib cage
→ waist
→ pelvis.

Do not turn these three regions into one uniform tube.

## Shoulders

Connect the arm to the rib cage through the shoulder mass.

Avoid attaching arms directly to the side contour.

## Limbs

Transition gradually between:

joint
→ muscle mass
→ joint.

## Overlap

Use overlap to communicate depth.

Near forms should visually overlap distant forms when appropriate.

## Rule

Construction is built AROUND the mannequin.

It does not replace the mannequin with an unrelated silhouette.
