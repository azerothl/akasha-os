# Human Proportions

Proportions are guides rather than absolute anatomical laws.

## Adult realistic figure

Typical height:

7.5 to 8 heads.

Useful approximate landmarks:

0 heads — top of skull

1 — chin

1.5 — shoulders

2 — chest

3 — navel / elbows

3.8 to 4 — pelvis / crotch / wrists

5.5 to 6 — knees

7.5 to 8 — ground

## Arms

Relaxed arm:

elbow approximately around waist level.

wrist approximately around pelvis/crotch level.

hand reaches approximately upper/mid thigh.

## Legs

The pelvis-to-ground region represents approximately half the figure.

## Shoulders

Typical adult shoulder width:

approximately 2 to 2.5 head widths.

Body type can alter this significantly.

## Important

Do not enforce idealized measurements blindly.

Preserve:

- intended body type
- age
- style
- perspective
- foreshortening

Perspective changes projected lengths but not anatomical bone lengths.
