# Clean Line Work

Clean line is extracted from the completed construction.

Do not redraw the character from imagination.

## Preserve

- pose
- anatomy
- proportions
- overlaps
- perspective

## Line hierarchy

Use stronger lines for:

- silhouette
- major overlaps
- important structural boundaries

Use lighter lines for:

- internal details
- subtle anatomical indications
- folds
- minor features

## Construction removal

Construction lines should be reduced progressively.

Do not remove them before the final contour has been established.

## Details

Only now refine:

- face
- fingers
- hair
- clothing
- accessories

## Rule

Details follow structure.

Structure never follows details.
