# Mannequin Construction

Convert the skeleton into simple three-dimensional primitives.

## Head

Use:

sphere or ellipsoid
+
jaw wedge.

Add center lines.

## Rib cage

Use:

egg-shaped volume

or

rounded box.

The larger portion is generally toward the upper chest.

Show orientation using cross-contours.

## Pelvis

Use:

box
bucket
or flattened rounded block.

The pelvis and rib cage must remain separate masses.

## Arms

Upper arm:

tapered cylinder.

Forearm:

tapered asymmetric cylinder.

Hand:

wedge.

## Legs

Thigh:

large tapered cylinder.

Lower leg:

asymmetric tapered volume.

Foot:

wedge.

## Joints

Use simplified spheres when useful.

## Cross-contours

Cross-contours communicate the direction of a volume in 3D space.

Use them especially on:

- rib cage
- pelvis
- arms
- thighs

## Rule

Every primitive must follow the underlying skeleton.

Never reposition a joint to make a primitive easier to draw.
