# Simplified Drawing Anatomy

This skill uses anatomy to clarify form.

It does not attempt medical anatomical illustration.

## Priority

Large masses before small muscles.

## Torso

Prioritize:

- neck
- trapezius
- pectoral/chest mass
- rib cage
- abdominal mass
- lat/back mass
- pelvis

## Arm

Prioritize:

- deltoid
- upper arm mass
- elbow structure
- forearm mass

## Leg

Prioritize:

- gluteal mass
- quadriceps mass
- hamstring mass
- knee structure
- calf mass

## Important

Muscles wrap around underlying volumes.

They should never flatten the mannequin.

## Rule

Anatomy REFINEs construction.

Anatomy does not redefine the pose.
